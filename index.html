SCALE · Seattle Composers Alliance Logbook & Estimator

A finance logbook for working artists that lives in one file and keeps your data on your device. Import bank statements, categorize with rules, forecast cashflow, estimate taxes, track hours and mileage, and generate invoices.

For individualsFreelancers, musicians, small businesses. Starts blank; your data stays in your browser. For organizationsProgram budgets, board reports, and treasurer tools for small and fiscally sponsored orgs. Individual demoPre-filled with fictional data. Safe to poke at everything. Organization demoA sample season of programs, budgets, and check requests.

Prefer an offline copy?

Download the file from the latest release, then open it in your browser (double-click it). That is the whole install.

Your data and privacy. Everything stays in your browser. No accounts, no server, no analytics. The only network activity: importing a PDF fetches the open-source PDF reader (pdf.js) from the cdnjs CDN, cryptographically verified before it runs, and any live sync you set up yourself talks only to your own Google Apps Script link. The online version and a downloaded copy keep separate data; pick one home, and Save & Restore backups move between them. Read the source or verify downloads against SHA256SUMS.txt.
